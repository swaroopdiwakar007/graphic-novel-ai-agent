# Panel layout conventions

- **Page size**: 6.625 x 10.25 in (US comic) unless the user says otherwise; portrait.
- **Panels per page**: 3-6 typical. A splash (1 panel) is for the biggest beat only. Max 9.
- **Reading order**: left to right, top to bottom. Keep gutters consistent (about 0.15 in).
- **Pacing**: more panels = slower/quieter moment; fewer, larger panels = impact.
- **Page turns**: end a page on a hook or reveal when possible.
- **Shot types**: establishing, wide, medium, close-up, extreme close-up, over-the-shoulder, bird's-eye, worm's-eye.
- **Continuity**: keep eyelines and screen direction consistent across panels (180-degree rule).
- **Lettering**: balloons top-left first; captions in boxes at panel top or bottom; SFX integrated into art. Leave empty space in the art for text.
